## §5 — Refinement branches generate the Cuntz/affine-braid algebra (positive)

The residue-branch isometries $R_{m,r}|q\rangle=|mq+r\rangle$ satisfy, exactly (verified):
\[
SR_{m,r}=R_{m,r+1}\ (r<m-1),\qquad SR_{m,m-1}=R_{m,0}S,\qquad V_m:=R_{m,0},\qquad \boxed{V_mS=S^mV_m,}
\]
and the Cuntz relation $\sum_{r=0}^{m-1}R_{m,r}R_{m,r}^\*=I$ (with each $R_{m,r}^\*R_{m,r}=I$ on the full
space; the finite-truncation test shows the partition-of-unity $\sum R_rR_r^\*=I$ cleanly). So the
"abstract" ax+b braid $V_mS=S^mV_m$ **is literally refinement ($R_{m,r}$) + carry ($S$ moving across the
branch boundary)**. The operator algebra generated by $S,\{R_{m,r}\}$ is the Cuntz–Toeplitz / ax+b
algebra. This makes precise the slogan **affine noncommutativity = carry curvature**: the single place
the relations are nonabelian is the carry step $SR_{m,m-1}=R_{m,0}S$ (the wrap that advances the quotient).
