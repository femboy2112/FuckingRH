## Critical qualifications discovered during porting

* The new global dimension is `(p-1)L`, not just `p-1`. Keeping only the new
  digit discards its entire old-history multiplicity.
* A rank-one twist at fixed L has a Möbius resolvent update. The full
  refinement L->pL has a degree-p rational update. These are different maps.
* The local prime-only cyclotomic product and the full mixed-conductor
  chronological determinant are different objects with different limits.
* A dense abstract rational affine group representation need not extend
  continuously to the real group. Counting-space and Lebesgue-space
  representations must not be identified.
* Neither a determinant-line ratio nor a pointwise convergent product of
  finite determinants automatically supplies a trace-class operator.

These are scope corrections, not reasons to discard the exact clock geometry.
