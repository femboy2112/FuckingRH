# A specified coherent clock/Gamma square

This is an independently specified candidate, not a factorization fitted to
a Weil matrix. It retains the continuum input and all LCM innovations. Its
failure is proved in the companion pushforward note; that failure is scoped
to the stated coupling class.

## 1. Canonical event vectors at the carrier horizon

List the prime powers `q_j=p_j^(k_j)<=N` increasingly, `j=1,...,m`.
Put `L_j=p_j L_(j-1)`, `L_0=1`, `L=L_m`, `d_j=L_j-L_(j-1)`.
Embed every earlier Haar clock in `H_L`, and let `E_j` be the difference
of its two successive old-space projections. All `E_j` are orthogonal
projections, their sum is the complement of the constant vector, and each
has constant diagonal `d_j/L` in the orthonormal point basis.

At the marked carrier origin choose `e0=sqrt(L)1_{0}` and define

\[
 c_j=\sqrt{d_j/L},\quad v_j=E_j e_0/c_j,\quad
 w_j=\log p_j/\sqrt{q_j},\quad a_j=\log q_j.
\]

Then `<v_i,v_j>=delta_ij`, `<e0,v_j>=c_j`, and
`sum c_j²=1-1/L`. The origin is a specified geometric choice; we do not
claim that arithmetic uniquely forces every part of this candidate.
Put `delta_j=tau_(a_j)-I` on `L²(R)` and

\[
 V_N f=\sum_j\sqrt{w_j}\,v_j\otimes\delta_j f.                \tag{C1}
\]

This is a bounded map at finite `N`, using no zero ordinates. Its squared
norm before interaction is the inherited event energy, by orthogonality.

## 2. Couple at the shared origin before squaring

Let `K=ell²(N_0)`, `H e_l=2l e_l`, and choose a normalized vector
`g_l=sqrt(1-r²)r^l`, with any declared `0<r<1` (the reproducible fixture
uses `r=1/2`). All Gamma ladder modes are retained. Let `Q=|e0><e0|` and

\[
 B_h=\exp[-h(Q\otimes H)]\otimes I_{L^2(\mathbb R)},\qquad h\ge0.
\]

This is the strongly continuous contraction semigroup generated by the
nonnegative self-adjoint `Q tensor H`. It acts on the event lift tensored
with `g`. Since `Q` does not commute with the individual conductor/event
projections, this is a genuine coherent interaction. It is not only an
external Gamma multiplier. Keep in addition the full, independently derived
Gamma difference operator `D_Gamma` from Round007. Define

\[
 T_{N,h}f=D_\Gamma f\ \oplus\ B_h(V_Nf\otimes g),\qquad
 Q_{N,h}(f)=\|T_{N,h}f\|^2.                                  \tag{C2}
\]

On the natural Gamma form domain, this is a closed operator: its first
component is closed and its second is bounded. Its graph block
`[[0,T*],[T,0]]` is a genuine self-adjoint odd operator on the usual domain.
The definition does not assert that its square is Weil.

Because `B_h²=(I-Q) tensor I+Q tensor exp(-2hH)`, its *complete* compression
is

\[
 Q_{N,h}(f)=E_N(f)-\kappa\|F_N f\|^2,\quad
 F_N=\sum_j c_j\sqrt{w_j}\,\delta_j,\quad
 \kappa=1-\frac{1-r²}{1-r²e^{-4h}}.                          \tag{C3}
\]

Here `E_N=||D_Gamma f||²+sum w_j||delta_j f||²` and `0<=kappa<1`.
The formula follows by evaluating the geometric series
`<g,exp(-2hH)g>`. The unshifted ladder `2l` matters: shifting it by `1/2`
changes this coefficient. The Gamma difference kernel separately uses
`H+1/2`, as required by Suzuki's normalization.

For arbitrary complex tests the polarized event term is

\[
 \sum_{i,j}\sqrt{w_iw_j}
       (\delta_{ij}-\kappa c_i c_j)
       \langle\delta_i f,\delta_j g\rangle.                 \tag{C4}
\]

Its positivity is independently manifest from (C2), or from
`I-kappa cc* >= (1-kappa||c||²)I>0`. Its off-diagonal entries have now
been retained and calculated before any scalar determinant or limit.

## 3. What completion remains missing

The continuous Gamma difference sector is included exactly; the `c0` identity
term and pole pairing are **not silently declared included**. They are the
explicit residual in `WEIL_PUSHFORWARD.md`. The construction is therefore a
candidate for a completed square, not a claim that completion has succeeded.
The heat interaction adds actual cross terms, but a derived mismatch kills
this candidate and the broader localized-boundary metric class.
