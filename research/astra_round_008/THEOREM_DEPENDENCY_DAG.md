## Dependency cut that survives

An independently specified **nonreducing** interaction and its exact
continuum pairing are missing. It must break the hypotheses of the
localized-site/unchanged-complement theorem and the compatible-oscillator
reducing-algebra theorem. Breaking a hypothesis is only admissibility for a
new attempt; it is not evidence that the new attempt is positive or equals
Weil. The required next test is the complete polarized discrepancy, before
determinants, zero calibration, or an infinite limit.
