# Round008 hole contract

**Object:** the normalized LCM clock filtration, its full mixed-conductor
innovations, and a retained continuum Gamma/test-function sector.
**Target:** an independently defined coherent operator whose exact completed
quadratic pairing is Weil, or a sharp obstruction to a specified constructor.
**Current state:** pathway gap at the coupling/pairing, not at the elementary
finite harmonic identities. RH remains open.

## Primary discriminators, declared before construction

1. **Global versus local determinant.** Compute full innovation determinants
   without removing mixed conductors. Pass: same arithmetic function with a
   proved admissible determinant notion. Fail: an exact coefficient or
   convergence-domain mismatch. Ambiguous: finite numerical closeness only.
2. **Small state.** Classify separately nonlinear scalar recursion, Möbius
   action, finite autonomous linear realization, and time-dependent transfer.
   Pass: closed equations with no desired endpoint inserted. Fail: rational
   degree/pole-count obstruction in a named class. No universal finite-state
   impossibility is inferred from one failed realization convention.
3. **Coherent boundary/Gamma square.** Preserve the full clock and continuum
   input, calculate cross terms before scalarization, then subtract Weil.
   Pass: exact equality or an independently positive/vanishing correction.
   Fail: a structural translated-kernel atom, rank, or bulk mismatch.
4. **Poisson interface.** Derive finite Gaussian/Fourier and refinement maps.
   Pass: commuting diagram with all normalization factors. Fail: exact
   mismatch, including the constant-vector control. Functional equation or
   self-duality alone is not a proof of RH.

Known guards: Round007 observation and residual theorems, forward determinant
no-go, and non-forcing of half-density by factor symmetry. Constructor
definitions use no zero ordinates. No finite PSD conclusion is promoted.

Novelty tax: finite harmonic analysis, cyclotomic factorization, affine
unitarity, Gamma determinants and Poisson summation are classical. Progress
must be a proved new coupling/pairing edge or an exact exclusion of a natural
implementation. Merely recasting the RH-equivalent sign is not a reduction.

Fresh holdouts will include nonlocal refinements (p not dividing L and p
dividing L), mixed conductors, random nested composite refinements, wrong
Haar normalization, changed phases/charges and removed Gamma. Proofs carry
universal quantifiers; exact/interval tests check witnesses and code.
