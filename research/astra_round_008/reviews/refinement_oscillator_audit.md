# Bounded audit: refinement-compatible carry/oscillator

Auditor: the Round008 Gamma fork, independently recomputing the root's
proposed maps. This is a same-model algebra check, not an independent
research provenance family. No git operation or central-file edit.

**Verdict:** all stated intertwining formulas and the corrected heat trace
are valid under the conventions below. A compatible continuum interaction
really exists. It is block diagonal in clock harmonics after the explicit
coordinate unitary. The valid obstruction concerns cross-sector pairings
inside that reducing algebra, not all possible metrics or operators.

## 1. Measures and maps

Use

\[
\mathcal K_L=L^2(\mathbb Z/L\mathbb Z,\text{normalized Haar})
 \otimes L^2(\mathbb R,dy),\qquad
\|f\|_L^2=\frac1L\sum_{a=0}^{L-1}\int|f_a(y)|^2dy.
\]

Put `M=pL`, and write every new lane uniquely as `c=a+bL`,
`0<=a<L`, `0<=b<p`. Define

\[
(S_{L,M}f)_{a+bL}(y)=\sqrt p\,f_a(b+py).
\]

Direct substitution `z=b+py` gives

\[
\|S_{L,M}f\|_M^2
=\frac1{pL}\sum_{a,b}\int p|f_a(b+py)|^2dy
=\frac1L\sum_a\int|f_a(z)|^2dz.
\]

The `sqrt(p)` is essential for these measures. Each lane's continuum
integration already covers all `R`; these are not disjoint intervals in
the quotient coordinate.

The carry unitary acts on lane functions as

\[
(U_Lf)_a(y)=
\begin{cases}f_{a-1}(y),&a>0,\\f_{L-1}(y-1),&a=0.
\end{cases}
\]

Then `U_M S=S U_L`. For `a>0` both sides give
`sqrt(p) f_(a-1)(b+py)`. For `a=0,b>0` they give
`sqrt(p) f_(L-1)(b+py-1)`. At the full new-clock wrap `a=b=0`,
`(p-1)+p(y-1)=py-1` gives the same value. This verifies the boundary as
well as interior lanes. Negative powers intertwine too, by multiplying
the unitary equality by the inverses.

## 2. Covariant oscillator and domains

On the finite direct sum of Schwartz spaces put

\[
(K_Lf)_a=-L^{-2}f_a''+ig((a+Ly)^2-1\big)f_a.
\]

The chain rule gives `K_M S=S K_L`: the second derivative of
`sqrt(p) f_a(b+py)` supplies `p²`, canceled by `(pL)^(-2)`, while
`a+bL+pLy=a+L(b+py)` identifies the potential.

Define the lane-wise unitary

\[
(R_Lf)_a(x)=L^{-1/2}f_a((x-a)/L).
\]

Its norm is unchanged by `x=a+Ly`. Its inverse is
`(R_L^*g)_a(y)=sqrt(L)g_a(a+Ly)`. Therefore

\[
R_LK_LR_L^*=I\otimes H_{\rm osc},\qquad
H_{\rm osc}=-\partial_x^2+x^2-1.
\]

Take the self-adjoint realization
`Dom K_L=R_L^*(direct-sum Dom Hosc)`, not a formal differential expression
with an unspecified extension. Schwartz vectors are a core. The oscillator
has compact resolvent and eigenvalues `2m`; this remains true with finite
clock multiplicity. Each affine coordinate map preserves Schwartz spaces.
Translation preserves the oscillator domain, and the refinement maps
intertwine the self-adjoint realizations and their bounded spectral
calculi by the coordinate model below.

## 3. The refinement and carry in physical coordinates

Let `J_(L,M)` be the normalized-Haar clock pullback
`(Jg)_(a+bL)(x)=g_a(x)`. Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
(R_MSf)_{a+bL}(x)
&=(pL)^{-1/2}\sqrt p\,
f_a\!\left(b+p\frac{x-a-bL}{pL}\right)\\
&=L^{-1/2}f_a((x-a)/L)=(JR_Lf)_{a+bL}(x).
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus `R_M S=J R_L`. In particular the refinements compose consistently
for `L|M|N`: `S_(M,N) S_(L,M)=S_(L,N)`.

For `C_L` the cyclic shift `(C_Lg)_a=g_(a-1 mod L)`,

\[
\boxed{R_LU_LR_L^*=C_L\otimes\tau_1.}
\]

At `a>0` the physical argument is
`a-1+L(x-a)/L=x-1`. At `a=0` it is
`L-1+L(x/L-1)=x-1`. Both branches yield the same physical translation.
There is no remaining `1/L` in that translation.

This distinction explains why the new trace differs from the fixed
quotient-oscillator control in `GAMMA_DRIFT.md`. The old control used
`I tensor Hosc` on the **quotient** variable; the compatible `K_L` uses the
physical coordinate `a+Ly`. They are different specified generators.

## 4. What reduces, despite noncommutation

The pair `C_L tensor tau_1`, `I tensor Hosc` does not commute, since
`[Hosc,tau_1]=(2x-1)tau_1`. Nonetheless every clock-harmonic projector
`P_chi tensor I` commutes with the first unitary and reduces the second
self-adjoint operator. The same is true of sums of harmonics: exact
conductor projections, inherited-clock projections, and innovation
projections.

Let `A_L` be the von Neumann algebra generated by `U_L,U_L*` and the
bounded spectral calculus of `K_L`. For the corresponding projections
`Q_i`, the preceding common commutation implies

\[
Q_i A=AQ_i\quad(A\in\mathcal A_L),\qquad
\langle Q_if,AQ_jg\rangle=0\quad(i\ne j).
\]

Indeed a projection's bounded commutant is closed under multiplication,
adjoint, and weak limits. For unbounded words the same conclusion holds
on a specified common invariant domain; using the bounded algebra avoids
unstated domain extensions. A positive operator or closed form obtained
by reducing spectral calculus also keeps these subspaces orthogonal in
its form domain.

**Do not strengthen this conclusion incorrectly.** On a harmonic of
eigenvalue `lambda_chi`, `U_L` is `lambda_chi*tau_1`. Thus a function of
the coupled generators can have phase-dependent diagonal energies. It
does not act identically in every harmonic. The theorem proves no
cross-harmonic/cross-innovation matrix elements in this algebra, not that
all diagonal energies are fixed or that arbitrary positive metrics cannot
change them. An origin readout, a source injection mixing harmonics, or a
noncommuting clock observable is additional structure outside the theorem.

The inductive-limit statement has the same scope: along the cofinal LCM
clocks the coordinate model is
`L²(profinite Z) tensor L²(R)`, with the odometer times real translation
and one common oscillator. The finite-conductor projections still reduce
these generators. No nontrivial conductor coupling appears just by taking
this inductive limit.

## 5. Exact heat trace and innovation trace

For `t>0`, the heat operator is trace class. The carry is bounded, so
unitary conjugacy and the tensor trace rule give

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tK_L}U_L^k)
&=\operatorname{Tr}(C_L^k)
  \operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tH_{\rm osc}}\tau_k)\\
&=L\,1_{L\mid k}\,
\frac{\exp[-k^2\coth(t)/4]}{1-e^{-2t}}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The Gaussian trace follows by the Mehler completion of the square already
proved in `GAMMA_DRIFT.md`. Haar normalization does not divide the operator
trace by `L`; trace counts orthonormal clock dimensions. A normalized trace
would be a separately declared observable.

At `M=pL`, inherited-clock reduction gives the innovation trace

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm{innovation}}
 (e^{-tK_M}U_M^k)
=\left(M1_{M\mid k}-L1_{L\mid k}\right)
\frac{\exp[-k^2\coth(t)/4]}{1-e^{-2t}}.}
\]

The prefactor is equivalently the sum of Ramanujan character traces over
the newly introduced conductors. At `k=0` it is `M-L`; if `L|k` but
`M` does not divide `k`, it is `-L`. A negative twisted trace is compatible
with a positive heat operator because the inserted unitary is not a
positive observable. It is not a negative Weil certificate.

This exact comparison supplies a shared Gaussian factor times the known
clock character trace. No completed Weil compression is established by
it. The next escape must specify the additional cross-conductor source or
readout and test its full pairing rather than infer it from noncommutation.
