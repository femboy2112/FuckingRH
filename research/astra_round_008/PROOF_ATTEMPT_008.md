## First unpaid theorem and the exact escape boundary

To continue toward RH, construct a refinement-compatible interaction that
does **not** reduce the arithmetic innovation sectors and changes enough
of the complementary metric (or supplies the necessary unbounded gain).
Then prove its full polarized continuum pairing equals the completed Weil
form, or differs by a controlled residual tending to zero. No operator with
that identity has been constructed. Saying 'a nonlocal square exists' is
still an RH-level demand, not a theorem reduced to known transport machinery.

A narrow next discriminator is to add a specified nonreducing clock
observable to the compatible family (RC4), and calculate its Duhamel/Gram
kernel before choosing any normalization. Test first whether the
log(3/2) translated component and the bulk identity coefficient can both
match the target. A nonzero forbidden atom or an unchanged complement
immediately rejects that constructor. A successful local cancellation
would still need all other coefficients and a convergence theorem.

No finite certificate, theta symmetry, classical functional equation,
spectral phase fit, or implicit desired square root is allowed to discharge
this unpaid theorem. RH remains open.
