## A. The affine braid already contains fractional SUCC (exact)

From `D_q T_a D_q^{-1} = T_{qa}` (verified symbolically) one reads off, with `a → a/q`:

    ┌──────────────────────────────┐        D_{1/p} T_1 D_p = T_{1/p}
    │   T_a D_q = D_q T_{a/q}        │        T_1 D_p = D_p T_{1/p}
    └──────────────────────────────┘        T_{-1} D_p = D_p T_{-1/p}

So moving a unit SUCC past an **inverse** dilation produces a **fractional** successor `T_{1/p}`. Since every
denominator factors into primes, the group generated by the unit translation and the prime dilations and
their inverses is all rational translations:

    ⟨ T_1 , D_p^{±1} : p prime ⟩  =  { T_r : r ∈ Q }.

Caution (kept from the directive): `S^{1/p}` is **not** an operator on `l²(N)`. The fractional steps live
*upstairs* as genuine translations `T_r` on `l²(Q)`; downstairs they are only visible through the braid.

## B/C. The ladder of completions and the infinitesimal algebra

`N → Z → Q → R` is **three** distinct completions, not one:

1. inverse SUCC: `N → Z` (bilateral additive carrier);
2. inverse FUCC + translation: `Z → Q` (rational, forced by `n/p`);
3. continuous SUCC: `Q → R` — **not** algebraic; the permutation rep on `l²(Q)` does *not* become a
   strongly continuous `R`-translation rep. A new analytic completion (e.g. `L²(R)`, an induced
   representation, or a direct integral) is required.

On `L²(R)` with `T_t = e^{-itP}`, `(T_t f)(x) = f(x-t)`, `P = -i d/dx`, and dilation generator
`A = -i(x d/dx + 1/2)`, the affine Lie relation is (verified symbolically):

    ┌──────────────────┐
    │   [A, P] = i P     │        with the group form   D_q T_t D_q^{-1} = T_{qt}.
    └──────────────────┘

The discrete braid `V_m S = S^m V_m` (our Round004/005 core) is the **lattice shadow** of this continuous
`ax+b` relation. "FUCC is a change of SUCC resolution" is this `[A,P]=iP`.

## D/G. The central conceptual reading: group completion ≠ analytic continuation

The factorial recursion `Γ(z+1) = z Γ(z)` (verified) is a *weighted-successor* intertwining
`T_1 Γ = M_z Γ`, and inverse SUCC `Γ(z) = Γ(z+1)/z` extends `Γ` leftward until the poles at `0,-1,-2,…`
obstruct it. But — and this is the point — **the recursion alone does not determine `Γ`.** Bohr–Mollerup:
`Γ` is pinned among all solutions of `f(z+1)=z f(z)`, `f(1)=1` only by the **extra analytic datum of
log-convexity** (equivalently Wielandt's boundedness). So:

> **Group/semigroup completion is algebraic; analytic continuation is not.** The affine group completion
> gives the recursion (the Euler/Dirichlet region `Re s > 1`), but the *continuation* into the critical
> strip requires an additional analytic selection principle that the algebra does not contain.

This is the **structural reading** of why the inverse-affine program lands on the Weil wall — a heuristic
argument from the `Γ` control, **not** a formal theorem (the sound, formal part is the *negative* claim that
the algebra alone reproduces `ζ` only in `Re s>1`; "this is why" packages that negative fact as an
explanation, and should be read as such):

- The completed affine representation reproduces `ζ`/`−ζ'/ζ` **only in `Re s > 1`** (§F below), i.e. exactly
  where the Euler product / Dirichlet series converges = where the semigroup presentation is literal.
- The continuation to the strip — where RH lives — is the **functional equation**, whose content is the
  **Archimedean Γ-factor + product formula**, i.e. the *extra analytic datum*, not derivable from the group
  completion. RH is a statement about the *continued* object's zeros, invisible to the algebra.

This matches Round005 C98 exactly ("the renormalization is analytic (continuation), not algebraic") and
the literature verdict (`LITERATURE_INTERFACE.md`): the Connes/Weil gap is precisely the missing analytic
positivity, not a missing algebraic object.

## E. Gamma = the Archimedean Mellin↔Fourier intertwiner (exact)

Mellin diagonalizes dilation; Fourier diagonalizes translation; `Γ` is the transition kernel between them:

    ∫_0^∞ x^{s-1} e^{-x} dx = Γ(s),      ∫_0^∞ x^{s-1} e^{±ix} dx = Γ(s) e^{±iπs/2}   (0<Re s<1, regularized).

Verified: at the self-dual point `s=1/2` this is the **Fresnel integral**,
`Γ(1/2) e^{iπ/4} = √(π/2)(1+i)` (both real and imaginary parts `= √(π/2)`, matched to 30 digits). The phase
`e^{iπs/2}` is the Archimedean local root number. So, with `SUCC ~` additive/Fourier and `FUCC ~`
dilation/Mellin:

> **`Γ` is the place-`∞` local zeta factor** — the Archimedean analogue of the finite-prime Euler factor
> `(1−p^{-s})^{-1}`, realized as the exact intertwiner between the additive and multiplicative pictures.
> This is Tate's local functional equation at the real place (`LITERATURE_INTERFACE.md §3`), not a new
> object; but it names the missing datum of §G concretely: the pole/`Γ`-sector is the analytic continuation
> of a Mellin matrix coefficient, **not** an ordinary positive spectral density — which is exactly why
> Round004/005 found the pole sector indefinite (C83/C91), living at "imaginary frequency."

## F. Prime clocks as phasors (exact, `Re s > 1` only)

    −ζ'/ζ(σ+it) = Σ_{p,k} (log p) p^{-kσ} e^{-i t k log p}    (classically exact, Re s>1; the finite

## K. Grünwald–Letnikov fractional difference and fractional ζ (exact + classical)

The augmentation/difference operator `I−S` has a functional-calculus fractional power

    (I − S)^α = Σ_{k≥0} (−1)^k C(α,k) S^k      (Grünwald–Letnikov),

verified on the finite nilpotent shift: `((I−S)^{1/2})² = I−S` exactly. The braid lifts to the fractional
power by functional calculus:

    D_q (I − T_t)^α D_q^{-1} = (I − T_{qt})^α        (since D_q f(T_t) D_q^{-1} = f(T_{qt})).

On Dirichlet terms, the GL node `lim_{h→0}(k^h−1)/h = log k` (verified) produces the fractional-zeta
heuristic

    ζ^{(α)}(s) "=" Σ_n (−log n)^α n^{-s} = Σ_n e^{iπα}(log n)^α n^{-s}      (Re s > 1),

verified for integer `α=1,2` (`ζ^{(m)}(s) = Σ(−log n)^m n^{-s}`, matched to `<10^{-10}`). This is the
Fehlau/Guariglia fractional derivative of `ζ` (see `FLAMMABLE_MATHS_PROVENANCE.md`). It is a **`Re s>1`
Dirichlet-region representation — RH-inert**: it brings down `(log n)^α` weights but, like §F, does not
reach the critical line, and the Gamma/Mellin leg (which *would* carry the continuation) is **not wired to
it** — exactly the §G gap, in fractional-calculus clothing.
