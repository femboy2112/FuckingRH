## Controls and stop rules

The hypotheses were chosen to distinguish the proposed mechanisms before accepting a positive result:

- Memory: two exact reversible systems with identical immediate shadows and opposite delayed return phase.
- Positive primitive extraction: retain mixed products versus erase both6 and3/2 correlations; check an exact rational witness, not just a numerical eigenvalue.
- Duality: preserve weight-one pairing and reversal in both examples; remove only positive dynamical compatibility in the negative control.
- Frobenius histories: count actual curves independently; compare against a reciprocal polynomial that passes all pairwise tests.
- Connes–Consani comparison: full origin/polar constants versus an omitted-origin mutation; use both unrestricted and two-moment-neutral smooth tests.
- Green reconstruction: genuine two-moment neutrality versus ordinary zero mean only.
- Clock: smooth parameter versus square-root scaling; separately integrate an actual history translation dilation.
- Infinite cutoffs and multiplier quotient: use analytic proofs rather than extrapolating finite computations.

Once these controls and the theorem audits passed, no extra numerical search for evidence of RH was undertaken.

## Reproduce

~~~bash
python research/2026-10-09/reversal_memory_polarization/evidence/donor_history_conditioned_reverse_probe.py
python scripts/reversal_memory_polarization_probe.py --output research/2026-10-09/reversal_memory_polarization/evidence/probe_results.json
~~~

The integer matrix and point-count calculations are exact. NumPy eigenspectra, special functions, and SciPy quadrature are diagnostic; floating-point error estimates are not certified mathematical bounds. The analytic proofs establish the general statements.
