# 1. Suzuki's finite-interval prime term

Let

\[
v\in H_0^1(-a,a)
\]

and extend \(v\) by zero to all of \(\mathbb R\).

Suzuki 2026, equation (2.5), contains the arithmetic term

\[
-\sum_{n\le e^{2a}}
\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
\left[
\int_{-a}^{a-\log n}
v(x+\log n)\overline{v(x)}\,dx
+
\int_{-a+\log n}^{a}
v(x-\log n)\overline{v(x)}\,dx
\right].
\]

The two integrals are complex conjugates.

Let

\[
(\tau_hv)(x)=v(x-h)
\]

on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\).

Then the bracket is

\[
\boxed{
2\Re\langle\tau_{\log n}v,v\rangle.
}
\]

Therefore the prime contribution is

\[
\boxed{
-2
\sum_{n\le e^{2a}}
\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
\Re\langle\tau_{\log n}v,v\rangle.
}
\]

Only prime powers contribute because \(\Lambda(n)=0\) otherwise.

# 2. Exact square completion

Translation on the full line is unitary:

\[
\|\tau_hv\|_2=\|v\|_2.
\]

Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|v-\tau_hv\|_2^2
&=
2\|v\|_2^2
-
2\Re\langle\tau_hv,v\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
-2\Re\langle\tau_hv,v\rangle
=
\|v-\tau_hv\|_2^2
-
2\|v\|_2^2.
}
\]

Apply this to every prime-power shift:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&-2
\sum_{n\le e^{2a}}
\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
\Re\langle\tau_{\log n}v,v\rangle
\\[1mm]
&\qquad=
\sum_{n\le e^{2a}}
\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
\|v-\tau_{\log n}v\|_2^2
-
2
\left(
\sum_{n\le e^{2a}}
\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}
\right)
\|v\|_2^2.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

Define the positive prime-ray Dirichlet energy

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{prime},a}(v)
=
\sum_{p^k\le e^{2a}}
\frac{\log p}{p^{k/2}}
\|v-\tau_{k\log p}v\|_2^2.
}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{prime},a}(v)\ge0
}
\]

unconditionally.
