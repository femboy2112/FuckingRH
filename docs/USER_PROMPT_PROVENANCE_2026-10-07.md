## 1. Short answer: what was the user's actual recurring question?

Across the sessions, the recurring request was not "find a nice analogy for zeta." It was:

> Can the visible successor history on natural integers, jointly with prime-factor/congruence clocks, be lifted to a coherent arithmetic interaction geometry whose exact global, Archimedean-completed quadratic pairing is Weil/Suzuki positivity?

This sentence is **editorial synthesis**, **not** a user quotation. It organizes the evidence, without asserting the existence of such a lift.

The user repeatedly separated:

1. a **causal carrier** (SUCC / successor pulse),
2. multiplicative **structural directions** (FUCC / prime axes / factor support),
3. **event history** (prime-power activation, residue clocks, carry, boundary crossings),
4. **representation** (shadow, cube, finite matrix, basis, twist, phase),
5. a **global completion** (Gamma, inverse/trivial-zero ladder, Archimedean place),
6. the demanded **truth test** (exact Weil form or Suzuki screw/Hankel object; no off-line zero conclusions from mere resemblance).

They also explicitly challenged whether a specific modulus map is physically meaningful or merely an encoding, and demanded a proof path rather than recursive analogizing.

## 3. Genealogy: the ideas were not invented all at once

### Strand A: prime-support combinatorics and geometry (July–August)

U01–U03 suggest an earlier model in which prime support/multiplicity are independent coordinates and "ordinary integers" occupy a lower-dimensional visible representation. This became agent mathematics in `archive/legacy/*` and the later valuation-lattice constructions. At this stage `SUCC` / `FUCC` as labels need not have existed.

### Strand B: the global completion and the square-host problem (August)

U02, U04–U05 and U07 show that Gamma, a global positive pairing, a square or determinant over additive/multiplicative information, and a genuinely independent construction were already the desired *outcome*. Agent dossiers later compared Weil positivity, Hodge/cohomological hosts, prolate selection, and prime/infinite-place cancellation. None became an unconditional RH proof.

### Strand C: signal/band interpretations (September)

U06 supplied a separate radio/antenna picture. Agent work translated the metaphor into Mellin/log-frequency and designed controls for generic matched-filter behavior. The preserved `archive/legacy/RIEMANN_BAND_RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md` is a controlled analogy study, not evidence that the zeros confer a practical RF advantage.

### Strand D: SUCC–FUCC machine (October 5–6)

U08–U18 convert the arithmetic model into explicit computation: a causal successor pulse, multiplicative structural directions, coupled clocks, carries, and compositional histories. Agent notes add the affine semigroup relation, prime-ray first returns, profinite odometer, KMS measures, Pascal matrices, and positive operators. **These are different mathematical representations with different scopes**; the user did not already prove them by asking the question.

### Strand E: lift, shadow, continuum and proof target (October 6–7)

U19–U36 ask for a causally growing system whose boundary history transports into a *complete* global object, not merely a heuristic phase or a re-expression of Euler. The cube/sphere, Gamma rotation, curvature and correlated-wave language are exploration tools. U23–U24 and U35–U36 impose the critical constraint: *derive the exact Weil/Suzuki pairing, prove the sign, and keep the origins honest*.

## 5. Warnings against retroactive authorship and proof drift

- **Do not call agent-generated terminology a verbatim user prompt.** Examples include `UBRPCT`, `unit-basepoint second-jet Gram`, `parent-independence lemma`, `Schur–Vitali` and `localized-boundary no-go`. These are research formulations, whether inspired by the user or not.
- **Do not identify "Gamma rotation" with a particular operator until a phase, basis, orientation and norm are specified.** No declared transport, no mathematical phase claim.
- **Do not identify "quantum-feeling" with quantum mechanics.** Noncommutativity, sums of amplitudes and indefinite forms can occur in ordinary mathematics.
- **Do not claim that a latest repo README is the latest research branch.** On 2026-10-07 `main` held consolidation through Round006; open draft PRs #5 (Round007) and #6 (Round008) contain subsequent audits and results, including *corrections* to the parent.
