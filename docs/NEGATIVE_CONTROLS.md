# Negative controls and killed shortcuts

This file exists to prevent attractive dead ends from being rediscovered as "proofs."

1. **Functional equation alone is insufficient.** Symmetry \(s\leftrightarrow1-s\) pairs off-line zeros; it does not exclude them.
2. **Positive/Fourier-self-dual local vectors are insufficient.** Explicit positive self-dual Gaussian mixtures can have Mellin multipliers with off-critical zeros.
3. **Naive primewise Gaussian transport is not positive globally.** The two-prime inclusion-exclusion control has negative quadratic coefficient near zero.
4. **Theta positivity is insufficient.** Arithmetic periodization repairs positivity and recovers the functional equation but not Weil positivity.
5. **Ordinary CLT is insufficient.** It produces the universal second-cumulant Gaussian and erases the residual whose sign is RH-bearing.
6. **Finite numerical zero agreement is insufficient.** A finite self-adjoint spectrum can fit low zeros while the limiting determinant fails or grows the wrong residual.
7. **Compact-window positivity is insufficient without an exhaustion theorem.** The positivity margin can collapse extremely fast as the window grows.
8. **Cholesky of an already-positive truncation is circular.** Independent positivity must be constructed without assuming the target sign.
9. **A single common phase/unitary gauge cannot repair mixed-prime positivity.** Prior checkpoint work killed this class.
10. **Finite adic data without the Archimedean coordinate can be structurally blind.** Collatz controls exhibit this explicitly.
11. **Adelic language is not automatic decoupling.** The diagonal rational embedding couples all coordinates globally.
12. **Strong PNT/Chebyshev error bounds may be RH in disguise.** Every tail estimate must be audited for circularity.
13. **"Quantum" wording is not evidence.** Only a declared Hilbert space, operator, state, and falsifiable mapping count.
14. **Ordinal \(\omega\) is not the Archimedean place.** Ordinal support encodings may be useful bookkeeping; the completions are categorically different.
15. **Lambert \(W\) is not a proof mechanism by itself.** Its legitimate role is rank/mass inversion such as \(N\log N\leftrightarrow M\).
16. **Same-model computational agreement is not independent triangulation.** Independent implementation/provenance is required for evidence claims.
17. **Finite verification of \(\Psi(t)\ge0\) is not RH.** Suzuki's criterion is global in \(t\); the infinite tail is exactly the theorem.
18. **Checkpoint convexity does not solve the tail.** One minimum per prime-power interval still leaves infinitely many intervals.
