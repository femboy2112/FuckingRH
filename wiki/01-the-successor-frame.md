# 1. The successor frame

*This page builds the framing from first principles. It is a point of view, not a theorem;
where it touches proved mathematics, the mathematics is standard and cited. The purpose is
to make the rest of the program legible.*

---

## 1.1 The object is the dynamics, not the index

The natural numbers are usually handed to us as a finished set `{0, 1, 2, …}`. This program
takes the opposite starting point: the interesting object is the **process that generates
them**, and the finished index is a shadow of that process.

The generator is the **successor map**:

```
SUCC : n ↦ n + 1
```

Counting is iterated succession: `3 = SUCC(SUCC(SUCC(0)))`. The claim we hold onto — and it
is a *framing* claim, not yet a mathematical one — is that **the chain of applications is
load-bearing, and the integer label is not**. "3" is a name for a particular walk along the
additive worldline; the walk is the thing.

This matters because the Riemann Hypothesis is not a statement you can read off any finite
stretch of the index. It is a statement about a *global* feature of the arithmetic, and the
bet of this frame is that it lives in the dynamics (how the walk composes, rescales, and
completes) rather than in the labels the walk produces.

## 1.2 Multiplication is rescaling the successor step (`●`)

Addition is "take another step." Multiplication is "take a step of a different size."

Write the `a`-fold successor as `SUCC^a` (a walk of `a` unit steps). Multiplication is the
**composition that rescales the step length**:

```
(SUCC^m) ● (SUCC^n)  =  SUCC^{mn}
```

`m · n` is not a new atom; it is the `n`-walk performed with each step rescaled by `m`.
Nested succession *is* multiplicative structure. The operator `●` is the frame's name for
"multiplication seen as a rescaling of the additive dynamics," and its natural scalar
invariant — the factor by which it stretches the step — is the ordinary modulus `|m| = m`.

This is the frame's version of a fact every analytic number theorist uses: the
multiplicative group of positive reals acts on functions by scaling, `f(x) ↦ f(mx)`, and the
characters of that action are the power functions `x ↦ x^s`. The **Mellin transform** is the
Fourier analysis of this scaling action, and `s` is its frequency variable. When we later
write `n^{-s}`, we are writing the response of the `●`-rescaling at frequency `s`.

## 1.3 Primes are where multiplicative rays meet the additive worldline

Prewire, from the multiplicative unit `|1⟩`, the rays generated by `●`:

```
|1⟩ → |p⟩ → |p²⟩ → |p³⟩ → …     for each prime p
```

These rays are the irreducible directions of the `●`-action (irreducible because of unique
factorization — a prime ray cannot be reached by composing shorter ones). The points where
these rays land back on the additive worldline are exactly the **prime powers** `p^k`, and
the natural weight that records "a ray of prime `p` passed through here" is the **von
Mangoldt function**:

```
Λ(n) = log p   if n = p^k,     0 otherwise.
```

So `log p` is not an arbitrary charge; it is the step-size of the `●`-ray for `p` (the
infinitesimal generator of scaling by `p`), and `Λ` is the frame's record of ray–worldline
incidence. Every "arithmetic side" in this program is a sum against `Λ`.

## 1.4 Two kinds of place: the grain and the whole

The same rational number can be measured two ways:

- The **finite places** — one for each prime `p` — measure *arithmetic grain*: the `p`-adic
  absolute value `|x|_p` is large when `x` is very divisible by `p`. These places see the
  local multiplicative structure one prime at a time.
- The **archimedean place** `∞` measures ordinary *size*: `|x|_∞ = |x|`, the usual modulus.
  It is the "zoom all the way out" view — you can see the whole worldline but you cannot
  resolve the prime grain.

They are bound together by the **product formula**, a conservation law:

```
∏_v |x|_v  =  1        for every nonzero rational x
```

(the finite places' contributions exactly cancel the archimedean one). In the frame's
language: *the local grain and the global size are two readings of one object, and they are
not independent — they are tied at the hip by a single identity.* This is the adelic picture
(Tate), and it is the reason "local" and "global" information about `ζ` cannot be separated
at will.

A recurring intuition in this program is a **local → global transition that is part of the
bookkeeping**: you walk the finite places generating real local data, and the archimedean
place is the limit you reach by letting the steps become infinitesimal and the atoms
arbitrarily large — the frame where "I can see everything but I can't zoom in." Making that
transition *coherent while preserving positivity* is, it turns out, exactly the RH-hard step
(see [page 6](06-state-of-the-program.md), and the dynamical reading of ζ in
[§5.1](05-what-is-zeta-here.md)).

## 1.5 The archimedean factor, built from the frame (no zeta)

The completed zeta function carries an archimedean factor

```
Γ_ℝ(s) = π^{-s/2} Γ(s/2).
```

In this frame it is not imported from `ζ`; it is the scaling-response of the **self-dual
Gaussian** `g(x) = e^{-πx²}` — the unique fixed point of the Fourier transform, and the
natural archimedean "atom":

```
∫₀^∞ e^{-πx²} x^s d^×x  =  ½ π^{-s/2} Γ(s/2)     (d^×x = dx/x, the scale-invariant measure)
```

This is a standard Tate-thesis local computation, reproduced in the frame's own terms in
`scripts/` (built zeta-free). The point is structural: **the archimedean place contributes a
specific, positive, Gaussian "atom," and it is the `●`-scaling Mellin transform of that
atom.** The trivial zeros of `ζ` are precisely the poles of this Γ-bulk; they are absorbed in
the completion and carry no RH content (see
[page 2](02-the-rh-equivalent-target.md)).

The *self-duality* is not decoration. `e^{-πx²}` is the unique fixed point of the Fourier
transform, so it is the archimedean analogue of the "unramified" local vector at a finite place
(the indicator `1_{ℤ_p}`, also its own Fourier transform). Both are the natural neutral element
of their place, and the product formula ([§1.4](#14-two-kinds-of-place-the-grain-and-the-whole))
is what binds the neutral choices across all places into one object. This is why the archimedean
factor is *forced*, not fitted — and why the program treats the archimedean place as a genuine
partner of the primes rather than a normalization nuisance ([§5.2](05-what-is-zeta-here.md)).

## 1.6 "Stage" versus the thing we must prove

A piece of vocabulary used throughout the ledger. Call *the stage* everything you can
construct inside the finished index — every finite computation, every functional-equation
symmetry, every number you can write down. RH is **not on the stage**: it is a statement
about where an infinite family of zeros sits, and no finite stage object decides it.

The frame's slogan for the generative content that is *not* mere stage is **shadow-succ**:
the part of the structure that only appears as the generating process acts — "the wavefront
hitting an event." Concretely, this cashes out as the demand that a construction be
**dynamical and mutation-sensitive**: it must respond to the actual arithmetic (the real
primes, the real `log p`, the real half-density) and *break* when you feed it fake
arithmetic. A construction that is happy on the stage alone — that survives fake primes — is,
by definition, RH-inert. This is the origin of the [hostile-control
discipline](07-methodology-and-discipline.md).
